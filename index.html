      Reading a radial

      • A radial is the bearing from the station. Winnipeg is on the 210 radial – it lies south-west of March.
      • To fly to March from a location on radial 210, track the reciprocal: 210 − 180 = 030.
      • March variation is 5° E. Magnetic = True − 5°. (The course table is used as-is here.)
      • Know every landmark within 25 NM – MATS expects you to help pilots find reporting points.
